# How to Rent a Calculator: Student Guide

Source text for the kiosk brochure/pamphlet. It matches what the kiosk actually does (Phase 14e: students scan their ID with their own phone). If prices change on the dashboard, update the **Fees** box.

---

## The kiosk at a glance
```
   LOCKER 1                                LOCKER 2
 [BLUE] [RED]     [  LCD SCREEN  ]      [BLUE] [RED]
                   [ phone QR sticker ]
                   [ coin slot  ]
```
- **BLUE button**: choose this locker.
- **RED button**: "I have closed the door". Locks the locker.
- **LCD screen**: tells you what to do next.
- **Phone QR sticker**: scan it with your phone to open the ID scan page.

## Before you start
- Bring your **school ID with its QR code** and a **phone with a camera**, connected to the **kiosk Wi-Fi** (its name is on the sticker).
- Bring **coins**: ₱1, ₱5, ₱10 or ₱20. **The kiosk does not give change.**
- You can rent **one calculator at a time**.

## Opening hours
- **Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM** (closed on weekends and holidays).
- You can **borrow only during opening hours**. You can **return** any time the kiosk is on.
- **The 3-hour clock pauses when the kiosk closes** and continues when it opens again. Example: borrow at **3:00 PM** → 2 hours today + 1 hour the next school day → **due 10:00 AM** the next school day.

## Fees
| | |
|---|---|
| Rental | **₱20 for 3 hours** (counted during opening hours) |
| Late return | **₱10 for every 1.5 hours (or part of it)** after the due time, counted **only during opening hours**. There is a **10-minute grace period**. |
| Late fees | Paid at the kiosk **before** the locker opens for your return |

## Borrowing a calculator
0. **Screen dark or showing `PRESS ANY BUTTON`?** The kiosk is saving power. Any button wakes it and goes straight to step 2.
1. **Check the screen.** It shows `PRESS TO START` and the status of each locker, for example `1:READY 2:N/A`.
   - **READY** = a calculator is available in that locker.
   - **N/A** = not available.
2. **Press any button.** The screen shows `SCAN ID ON PHONE` / `CODE 482913 1:55`. You have 2 minutes to send your ID.
3. **Open the scan page on your phone** by scanning the QR sticker on the kiosk with your phone camera.
4. **Take a photo of your school ID.** Tap **Take or upload a photo** and take a close, sharp photo of the QR on your ID. Check that your name is shown.
5. **Type the 6-digit code** from the kiosk screen and tap **Send to kiosk**.
6. **Choose a locker.** The screen shows `PUSH BLUE BUTTON`. Press the **BLUE** button of a locker marked **READY** within 30 seconds.
7. **Pay ₱20.** The screen shows your name and `P20 / 3 HOURS`. Insert coins until it reads `PAYMENT P20/P20`.
8. **Open the locker.** The screen shows `LOCKER 1 OPEN` / `TAKE CALCULATOR`. Open the door and take the calculator.
9. **Close the door, then press RED.** The screen shows `CLOSE THE DOOR` / `PUSH RED BUTTON`. Shut the locker door firmly, then press that locker's **RED** button.
10. **Done!** The screen shows `RENTAL SUCCESS` and your due time, for example `DUE AT 01:00 PM`, or `DUE WED 10:00 AM` if it falls on another day. Note it down.

## Returning a calculator
1. **Press any button and send your ID from your phone**, exactly like borrowing steps 2–5. The kiosk knows you have a calculator out.
2. **If you are late,** the screen shows how late you are and the fee, for example `LATE 1H00M` / `PAY PENALTY P10`. Insert the coins. The locker opens only after the fee is paid.
3. **Open the locker.** The screen shows `RETURN CALC #1` / `TO LOCKER 1`, so you know which locker to use.
4. **Put the calculator back** in the same locker it came from.
5. **Close the door, then press RED.** When the screen shows `CLOSE THE DOOR` / `PUSH RED BUTTON`, shut the door and press that locker's **RED** button.
6. **Done!** The screen shows `RETURN SUCCESS` / `THANK YOU`, or the late fee you paid.

## Tips for scanning your ID
- Use good light. In a dim room turn on your phone camera's flash.
- Bring the phone close so the QR fills the frame, hold it steady, and avoid glare from the ID's plastic.
- Wipe a dirty ID first. If it says no QR was found, take the photo again a little closer.
- Your photo stays on your phone. Only the text in the QR (name, student number, course) is sent, and the kiosk keeps it as a record.

## What the screen means
| Screen | What to do |
|---|---|
| `PRESS ANY BUTTON` (dim screen) | The kiosk is sleeping to save power. Press any button to wake it. |
| `PRESS TO START` | Press any button to get a code, then send your ID from your phone. |
| `SCAN ID ON PHONE` / `CODE 482913 1:55` | Type this code on the phone scan page. The time left is shown. |
| `TIME RAN OUT` / `PRESS TO RETRY` | The code expired. Press a button for a new one. |
| `PRESS TO RETURN` / `OPEN MON 9:00AM` | The kiosk is closed. You can still return; borrowing opens at the time shown. |
| `KIOSK CLOSED` | You tried to borrow outside opening hours. |
| `DUE WED 10:00 AM` | Your due time is on another day (the clock paused overnight). |
| `PUSH BLUE BUTTON` | Press the blue button of a READY locker. |
| `LOCKER 2 N/A` | That locker is empty. Choose the other one. |
| `INSERT COINS` | Keep inserting coins until fully paid. |
| `TAKE CALCULATOR` | Open the door and take the calculator. |
| `CLOSE THE DOOR` / `PUSH RED BUTTON` | Shut the door, then press that locker's red button. |
| `WRONG CALCULATOR` | That isn't the calculator you borrowed. Put the right one in, then press red again. |
| `CALCULATOR NOT DETECTED` | Place the calculator properly inside, then press red again. |
| `ACTIVE RENTAL` / `RETURN IT FIRST` | You still have a calculator. Return it first. |
| `NO CALCULATOR AVAILABLE` | All calculators are out. Try again later. |
| `PAYMENT TIMEOUT` / `NO LOCKER PICKED` | You took too long. Press a button and start again. |
| `INVALID ID` | That was not a school ID QR. Press a button and try again. |
| `ACCOUNT BLOCKED`, `UNPAID PENALTY` or `SEE AN OFFICER` | Please contact an officer. |

## Reminders
- Always press **RED** after closing the door. If you don't, the locker locks by itself after about a minute, and an officer is notified.
- Return the calculator to **the same locker** it came from.
- Coins inserted when the kiosk is not asking for payment are **not credited**. Tell an officer for a refund.
- Don't force the door or press the buttons of a locker you are not using.
